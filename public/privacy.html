Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 10, 2026

Éternelle ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of our hosts and guests.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Éternelle application, we collect information you provide directly:

  • Event Data: Event dates, couple names, venue addresses, itinerary details, and registry links.
  • Guest Responses (RSVP): Guest names, email addresses, attendance status, dietary restrictions, meal selections, and optional song requests.
  • Account Details: Host name and email address for account authentication and event management.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data solely to deliver, maintain, and enhance the digital invitation and RSVP management service for hosts and their invited guests. We never sell or monetize personal guest information to third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Data is transmitted securely over HTTPS with industry-standard encryption protocols. We implement strict technical and organizational safeguards to ensure data protection.
