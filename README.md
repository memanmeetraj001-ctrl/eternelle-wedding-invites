# Éternelle — Luxury Interactive Digital Wedding Invitations

> Couture-grade digital wedding invitations & micro-sites featuring 3D wax seal reveals, live schedule timelines, dietary RSVP tracking, Google Maps directions, and Pinterest viral marketing tools.

## ?? Features
- **3D Animated Envelope Opening Experience**: Interactive wax seal tap reveal with custom background acoustics.
- **Editorial Typography & Colorways**: Inspired by Vogue, Kinfolk, and luxury European estates.
- **Guest RSVP & Catering Command Center**: Real-time RSVP management, dietary preferences, and 1-click CSV export.
- **Save-the-Date & Social Studio**: Formatted messages for WhatsApp, SMS, and Instagram Story announcements.
- **Pinterest Growth & Automation Hub**: Automated 9:16 video and vertical static pin generator with tailored SEO metadata.
- **Seamless Gumroad Checkout**: In-app Gumroad modal overlay with real-time plan upgrading.
